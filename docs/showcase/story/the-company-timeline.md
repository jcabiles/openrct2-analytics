# The company and its timeline

**Theme Park Enterprise is the project's name for how the game is played here: one company runs several parks on one shared calendar and reports them as a single business. The park company is that company. It starts on 1 March 2000 on its own calendar and plays by four rules: pay per ride, parks open one at a time, finished parks run on autopilot, and no repeat guests.**

<!-- passage: ent-rules-label -->
The company’s four business rules

<!-- passage: rule-1-h -->
Pay per ride

<!-- passage: rule-1 -->
Entry is free; rides and stalls charge.

<!-- passage: rule-1-eff -->
So every dollar comes from a ticket or a stall sale, and entrance revenue stays at $0.00.

<!-- passage: rule-2-h -->
Parks open one at a time

<!-- passage: rule-2 -->
Each park is built by hand until it is profitable and meets its scenario challenge (the game’s goal for that park). The next one opens the following March.

<!-- passage: rule-2-eff -->
So growth has two versions: like-for-like and as reported.

<!-- passage: rule-3-h -->
Automated runs for finished parks

<!-- passage: rule-3 -->
A finished park runs headless (the game plays alone, no window), one company year at a time.

<!-- passage: rule-3-eff -->
So old parks keep producing data with no player at the controls.

<!-- passage: rule-4-h -->
No repeat guests

<!-- passage: rule-4 -->
Leaving the park deletes the guest.

<!-- passage: rule-4-eff -->
So retention is not a metric here. Guest visits work like daily and monthly active users (DAU and MAU) in a software product.

<!-- passage: year-h -->
The company year runs March to October

<!-- passage: year-body -->
A year’s reports publish only after every park has finished it.

<!-- passage: months-label -->
The twelve calendar months: March to October are the eight company months; November to February are left out because the game has no such months.

<!-- passage: months-note -->
The game has no November to February, so the company year skips them.

<!-- passage: clock-day -->
One in-game day; the unit used for park and ride readings.

<!-- passage: clock-date -->
The company calendar, from 1 March 2000.

<!-- passage: clock-year -->
One eight-month season; the north star is reported per company year.

<!-- passage: clock-real -->

<!-- passage: clock-wry -->
Business figures use company dates or years; performance results use real time or game ticks.
