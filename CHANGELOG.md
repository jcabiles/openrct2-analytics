### Added

- Left Sandbox (throwaway practice) parks out of the dashboard's All parks figures by default, with a switch under the park picker ("Sandbox parks: Left out / Included") that brings them back; the choice lives only in the page address (`?sandbox=shown`) and the server passes park labels to the page through `window.DASHBOARD_LABELS`. - Showed, on the Executive page beside the company year chips, each park's game year for the shown company year and month ("Company year 2002 = game year 7"; a park not yet open in that month is named with no year): the dashboard data file gives each park a `game_years` entry per company year, with the game calendar year and the first company month number.
- Added a third answer to the launcher's question, "Run autopilot on a finished park": it lists the parks that can run, asks the `READY` phrase (ADR 0049) on a park's first run, records, runs `make autopilot-year` under `caffeinate -i`, files the year with `make auto-file DATA_SET=company COUNT_AS=company` and opens the dashboard; a park list that cannot be read gives `make autopilot-parks DATA_SET=company` and says to choose 3 again, a failed record step says to choose 3 again, and a year that does not finish says to choose 3 again when its messages show nothing was written or company data was not touched (or make stopped with a line starting with `***`), and otherwise gives the `make auto-file DATA_SET=company COUNT_AS=company` command to file by hand, because the year may have landed; a failed filing gives the same command.
- Warned about play recorded but never filed: `make unfiled DATA_SET=company` lists the parks with play waiting, the launcher shows the list and offers to file it before "Where should this play count?", and the dashboard banner says how many parks have play waiting, or play that can't be filed right now when the app will not offer it, or play that may still be waiting when the page shows older data, so its count comes from older data (the launcher's line after a failed filing says a rerun command is not needed); a session that failed its seq audit is named and never offered.
- Filed a play that opened several new parks in one `make auto-file` run: each new park is asked (its name, played game days, then "Opened first", "Opened second" or "Sandbox"), the order answers are checked against the first recorded sessions, every refusal check runs for all parks before the first registration, and the company parks are then registered one at a time, each with its own start year, before one after-play run.
